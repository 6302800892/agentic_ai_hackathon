# Risk Register

**Scales.** Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) are rated 1–5. Residual risk is what remains after the committed
control.

**Categories.** OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025) plus the NIST AI RMF function that owns the control.

**Owners are roles.** Assign names in the team's README.

| ID | Risk | Category | L | I | Mitigation → committed control | Residual | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Prompt injection in patient free text changes agent behaviour | OWASP LLM01 · NIST MANAGE | 4 | 5 | Quarantine wrapper and neutralisation in `src/context/quarantine.py`, `DATA_RULE` in every system prompt, block decision in `src/guardrails/input_guard.py`; tested in `tests/test_guardrails.py` and 15 attacks in `reports/redteam_results.json` | Low (novel phrasings can evade the patterns; the LLM Guard scanner is optional) | Security lead |
| R-02 | Cross-patient data access ("show me SYN-P-… record") | OWASP LLM02 · NIST MANAGE | 3 | 5 | Ingress cross-patient detection (`src/guardrails/input_guard.py::ingress`), HMAC session token checked server-side (`mcp_server/store.py::authorize`), `access_denied` audit records in `logs/agent_actions.jsonl`; `tests/test_tool_contracts.py::test_error_access_denied_for_other_patient` | Low | Security lead |
| R-03 | PHI written to logs, traces or answers in plaintext | OWASP LLM02 · NIST MEASURE | 3 | 5 | Ingress masking (`src/guardrails/phi.py`), `mask_obj` on every log write, `MaskingExporter` on every span (`src/observability/tracing.py`), output masking; scanned by `scripts/verify_citations.py` and `tests/test_guardrails.py::test_committed_evidence_has_no_plaintext_phi` | Low (Presidio NER misses some names; the synthetic formats are always caught) | Privacy lead |
| R-04 | Urgent / red-flag case mis-routed to routine booking | NIST MAP/MANAGE · safety | 3 | 5 | Safety-first routing (`src/agents/supervisor.py::decide`), rule safety net over the model (`src/agents/intake_classifier.py::apply_safety_net`), forced clinician in `src/guardrails/output_guard.py`; `tests/test_routing.py`; escalation recall gate in `reports/eval_report.json` | Low–Medium (red-flag vocabulary is finite; see `docs/model-card.md`) | Clinical safety lead |
| R-05 | The copilot offers a diagnosis or medication advice | OWASP LLM09 · safety | 3 | 4 | No-diagnosis system prompts (`src/context/select.py`), diagnosis-language rewrite (`src/guardrails/output_guard.py::contains_diagnosis`), clinical questions escalated; GEval `NoDiagnosisSafeRouting` in `scripts/run_eval.py` | Low | Clinical safety lead |
| R-06 | Hallucinated or invented policy used to justify a next step | OWASP LLM09 · NIST MEASURE | 3 | 4 | Retrieval-in-the-loop with a rule-checked stop (`src/agents/care_pathway.py`), `pathway_id="NONE"` → escalate, citation check against retrieved chunk ids (`src/guardrails/output_guard.py`); DeepEval hallucination + faithfulness in `reports/eval_report.json` | Low | ML lead |
| R-07 | Wrong coverage decision (eligibility decided by the model) | NIST MAP · business | 2 | 4 | Coverage decided deterministically by `mcp_server/store.py::check_coverage` (COV-R1..R6); gaps reported with rule ids; `tests/test_tool_contracts.py::test_check_coverage_output_schema_and_rules` | Low | Product owner |

| R-09 | Cost / latency runaway (token blow-up, slow tools) | OWASP LLM10 · NIST MEASURE | 2 | 3 | Light model for classification/grading/summaries (`src/runtime.py::make_gemini`), summarization middleware (`src/context/summarization.py`), chunk truncation (`src/context/compress.py`); monitored in `reports/golden_signals.json` | Low | ML lead |
| R-10 | Gemini or MCP server outage breaks intake | NIST MANAGE · availability | 3 | 3 | Timeouts + retries (`src/runtime.py`, `src/tools/logging_middleware.py`), rule-based fallback, in-process tool fallback (`src/service.py`), escalate-on-failure, circuit breaker (`src/runtime.py`); `tests/test_loops.py::test_tool_failure_cascades_to_escalation_not_retry_loop`, `tests/test_loops.py::test_llm_circuit_breaker_stops_hammering_a_failing_model` | Low | Platform lead |

| R-11 | Memory poisoning: an injected "fact" persists and influences later sessions | OWASP LLM04 · NIST MANAGE | 2 | 3 | Blocked inputs never reach `memory_write` (`src/graph.py`), only extracted non-clinical facts are stored, masked, namespaced per patient (`src/memory/long_term.py`); erasure via `python -m src.cli forget` | Low–Medium | ML lead |

| R-15 | Silent quality regression after prompt or model changes | NIST MEASURE | 3 | 3 | Golden-set eval with gates (`scripts/run_eval.py`), agent tests, single-command regeneration (`scripts/regenerate_all.py`) | Low | ML lead |
